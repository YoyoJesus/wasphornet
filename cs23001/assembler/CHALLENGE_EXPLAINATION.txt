- To Run my challenge work please use make assemblerprefix & then run ./assemblerprefix inputfile [optional output]
- Alternatively you can also run make prefix & then run ./prefix inputfile [optional output]

Answers to Questions;

1. Is it more complex than evaluating postfix? Why?
- Prefix is generally more complex than evaluating posfix since it requires that we iterate through
  the parts in reverse (right to left), and it is often needed to pop both operands when we encounter
  an operator. The back-to-front nature of this conversion makes it considerably less intuitive tha
  the postfix conversion.

2. Is the resulting assembler more complex?
- Both assemblers are pretty much the same, with slight differences between how operands and operators
  are loaded and applied. Prefix may have more operators applied back to back compared to postfix. The
  main complexity differences lie in the layout of the operands and their respective operators, the two
  processes are fundamentally the exact same.
